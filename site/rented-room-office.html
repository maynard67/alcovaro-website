Small-space living / Rented rooms

A workspace that can move with you.

When your office shares a bedroom or living room, the best first move is to organise what you already own. Plan a clear work surface, a cable route and somewhere to put work away.

Start without fixing anything to the wall

Try a tray for loose stationery, a box for chargers and a freestanding organiser for paperwork. Keep frequently used items within reach without filling the keyboard and mouse area. Measure storage before buying it: a basket that fits under a desk may still take up your legroom.

Plan the cable route before accessories

Place equipment near a suitable socket where possible. Check the manufacturer's instructions before routing or securing cables. Leave enough slack for devices to move, keep walking routes clear and do not route cables where a chair will roll over them. For electrical loading, extension leads and equipment condition, follow the manufacturers' safety instructions or obtain qualified advice.

Be careful with promises of damage-free mounting

Adhesive hooks and clips can still mark or lift a finish. Check the product's surface requirements and removal instructions; check your tenancy terms and obtain permission where needed before altering the property. A clamp is not automatically suitable for every desk either.

Create an end-of-day routine

  1. Put small accessories into one tray.
  2. Store paperwork in one labelled folder or box.
  3. Shut down equipment according to its instructions and put cables where they cannot be trapped.
  4. Leave the chair and walking route clear.

Buy only after a week of using the space

Notice what repeatedly gets in your way. One specific problem, such as nowhere to store a notebook, is a better reason to buy an organiser than the wish for a completely new setup. Start with the smallest change that solves it.

Before buying: measure the available space, check installation requirements and review return costs. This guide contains no affiliate links or paid recommendations.
