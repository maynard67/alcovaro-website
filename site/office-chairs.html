Guide 02 / Office chairs

Give your chair room to work.

In a smaller room, a chair's overall footprint matters as much as its appearance. Compare its dimensions with your desk and the space behind it.

Check the dimensions together

Look at the chair's overall width, base diameter, seat-height range and armrest height. Check whether it can tuck beneath the desk and whether drawers or crossbars obstruct it.

Compare the adjustments

Read the manufacturer's description of the seat, backrest and armrest adjustments. When possible, try a chair before committing. A product described as ergonomic is not a guarantee that it will suit every person.

Think about everyday use

  • Leave space to sit down, stand up and move away from the desk.
  • Check the stated user weight limit and assembly instructions.
  • Confirm that the castors suit your floor, or consider suitable floor protection.
  • Review the trial and return conditions, including return shipping costs.
Keep your desk measurements to hand while comparing chairs. A chair and desk should be considered together.

This guide covers space planning, rather than personalised health or posture advice.
