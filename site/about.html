Our approach

More thought per square metre.

Alcovaro is a developing resource for people making a workspace in a smaller home. Our focus is compact desks, office seating, monitor arms, storage and practical accessories.

Measure. Compare. Choose.

We want to help readers ask useful questions before buying: will it fit, will it work with what I already own, and will I have room to use it?

Where we are today

Alcovaro is in early access. We do not sell products, process payments or handle deliveries. We are preparing a supplier-fulfilled furniture shop. Customers would buy from Alcovaro and an approved supplier would deliver. Supplier arrangements are being explored; orders are not open.

Our planned shop

Before opening, we will publish available products, prices, delivery areas, returns information and seller details. Our guides remain available while we prepare the shop. We distinguish research from first-hand testing.
