Guide 03 / Monitor arms

Clear the desk. Check the details.

A monitor arm can free up surface space, but only when the screen, mount and desk are compatible. Start with the exact model of your monitor.

Check your screen

Find its weight without the stand and its mounting pattern in the manufacturer's documentation. Compare both with the arm's supported range. Screen size alone is not enough to establish compatibility.

Check your desk

Confirm the top's thickness, construction and suitability for the proposed clamp or grommet mount. Inspect the underside for frames, drawers or obstructions. Do not assume that glass or hollow-core desks can support a clamp mount.

Check the movement

  • Allow room behind the desk for the arm to fold or rotate.
  • Check the reach and height range against your intended setup.
  • Plan cable length with enough slack for movement.
  • Follow the screen, desk and arm manufacturers' installation instructions.
If the desk's suitability is unclear, ask its manufacturer before installing a mount.
