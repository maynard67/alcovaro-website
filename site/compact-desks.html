Guide 01 / Compact desks

A desk that earns its space.

A compact desk needs to fit both the room and the way you work. Before comparing styles, map out the space available and everything you need to use on the surface.

Measure the whole working area

Note the available width, depth and height. Include skirting boards, sockets, radiators, doors and any cupboards that need to open. Allow space to pull out your chair and move around it.

Plan the surface

Lay out your keyboard, mouse, screen and any notebooks on an existing table. Measure the arrangement. A shallow desk may fit an alcove but leave too little room for your equipment.

Check before ordering

  • Compare the assembled dimensions with your measurements.
  • Check knee space, drawer positions and the chair's armrest height.
  • Check the stated load limit and material, especially if fitting a monitor arm.
  • Measure the delivery route, including stairs and tight turns.
  • Read the retailer's assembly, delivery and return information.
Buying checklist: room measurements, equipment layout, chair clearance, access route and cable plan.

Choose a fixed desk or an adjustable one?

A fixed desk keeps the decision simple when space is tight. For a height-adjustable option, check the usable height range, cable slack and everything above or below the moving surface. A desk fitting the floor plan does not establish that it will fit your work equipment or suit you personally.
