Privacy / Updated 5 November 2026

Your information, explained.

Alcovaro is operated by Ben Maynard, trading as Alcovaro. For privacy questions or requests, email enquiry.alcovaro@gmail.com.

When you browse

This site is hosted by Netlify. Delivering and securing a website involves processing technical connection information, which can include an IP address, browser information, requested pages and timestamps. Netlify processes information under its own service arrangements and policies. Read the Netlify privacy policy.

We have not added advertising trackers, analytics tags, visitor accounts, contact forms or a newsletter signup. Our own site code does not set cookies or use browser storage. Hosting features may add their own components; this statement is not a claim that no third party processes visit information.

When you email

We receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include. We use this to answer questions, review corrections and handle partnership or privacy enquiries. We use Gmail for the mailbox; read the Google privacy policy. Please include only what is needed and do not send card details, identity documents or sensitive personal information.

Why we use information

Our purposes are responding to enquiries and running a reliable, secure website. For these activities, we rely on our legitimate interests in managing correspondence and operating the site, balanced against the rights and interests of the people concerned. If an enquiry leads to a contract or a legal obligation, different grounds may apply and will be explained where relevant. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

How long information is needed

Enquiry information is needed while the enquiry is being handled and for any necessary follow-up. After that, the need to retain it depends on whether there is an ongoing relationship, an unresolved complaint or dispute, or a legal recordkeeping requirement. Hosting and email providers also apply their own retention arrangements. Contact us if you want to ask about a particular message or request deletion.

Providers and links

Hosting and email providers process information to supply their services. Their processing may involve locations outside the UK; see their policies for details of their international processing arrangements. Clicking a retailer link takes you to a separate website under that retailer's privacy and cookie practices. No affiliate tracking links are active on Alcovaro at present.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, object to processing, or request restriction or portability. Contact us using the email above. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office.

Current launch status

The website is in early access. A public business correspondence address is still to be confirmed. We will update this notice if the site adds forms, analytics or affiliate tracking.
