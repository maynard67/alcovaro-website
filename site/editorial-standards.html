Editorial standards

Useful advice starts with clear evidence.

Alcovaro is operated by Ben Maynard, trading as Alcovaro. Our guides help UK readers plan smaller home workspaces. Here is the approach behind the current content.

Research is identified as research

Our current product examples are based on published retailer and manufacturer information. We have not physically tested the products mentioned. We do not describe research as a hands-on review, award star ratings or invent customer experiences.

We explain the space trade-offs

A desk can fit an alcove and still leave too little room for a chair or equipment. Our guides consider the whole setup: width, depth, access, legroom, installation and compatibility. Arithmetic examples are identified as illustrative rather than presented as measurements of a tested product.

Sources and changing information

Where we quote a product dimension, we link to the retailer or manufacturer and state when the listing was checked. Specifications, configurations, stock and prices can change. Confirm the exact variant and terms with the retailer before ordering.

Commercial relationships

We are exploring supplier relationships for a planned furniture shop. No supplier partnership is confirmed and no products are sold here. Our guides contain no active affiliate links or paid placements. Future commercial relationships will be clearly explained. We will distinguish our own products from external reference examples.

Corrections

If you spot an error, email enquiry.alcovaro@gmail.com with the page title and supporting information. We will review the issue and correct factual errors when confirmed.
